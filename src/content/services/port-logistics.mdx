A vessel call depends on dozens of shore-side movements happening in the right order, at the right time. Oar coordinates the supplier, documentation, storage and transportation steps that sit between a cargo requirement and a vessel delivery, so the operation moves as one coordinated process rather than several disconnected handoffs.
